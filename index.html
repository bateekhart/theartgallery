04 Our approach

It startedwith a stairwell.

Founded in 2020 by award-winning visual artist Rana Khalil, the ART GALLERY opened its first space around a feature stairwell, turning every climb into a curated journey through a theme.

The stairwell was never the point. The journey was.

Today we take that journey anywhere: into art districts, homes, offices, hotels and developments. Wherever the art needs to be, we curate the experience around it.

  1. 01

    Any space

    A stairwell, a downtown cinema, a residential lounge or an office floor. We read the space and build the journey through it.

  2. 02

    Any medium

    Painting, bronze, film manipulation, mosaic on satellite dishes, upcycled metal, installation. If it moves people, it belongs.

  3. 03

    Any scale

    From an intimate four-artist show to open calls with more than forty voices, every exhibition gets the same care.

  4. 04

    Anyone

    First-time exhibitors beside established names, and collectors beside first-time visitors. Art is for everyone, everywhere.
